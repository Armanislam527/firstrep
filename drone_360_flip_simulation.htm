Maneuvers

All motors equal thrust. Net force cancels gravity, no rotation.
Left motors vs right motors go to opposite extremes. Drone inverts sideways.
Front motors vs rear motors go to opposite extremes. Drone somersaults.
Thrust stays balanced (altitude holds); only CW/CCW reactive drag torque differs. No inversion.
■X = your ground x-axis (roll) ■Z = your ground y-axis (pitch) ■Y (up) = your vertical z-axis (yaw)
Hovering — all four rotors balanced, total thrust equals weight.
Why altitude dips during a flip: total thrust magnitude stays constant (two motors high + two low, the whole time) — but as the airframe rotates, that thrust vector rotates with it. Near 180° the thrust points toward the ground and adds to gravity instead of opposing it. Watch the world-space thrust arrows and the altitude readout above. Faster flips (higher "aggressiveness") spend less time inverted and lose less height — that's why acro pilots snap flips quickly.
